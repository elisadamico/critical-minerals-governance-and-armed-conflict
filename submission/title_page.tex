\section*{Statements and Declarations}

\subsection*{Ethical considerations}

Not applicable. The study uses only publicly available, aggregated secondary data
on conflict events and mineral exploration, and involves no human participants,
human data or human tissue. Ethical approval was therefore not required.



\subsection*{Declaration of conflicting interest and funding }

The author(s) declared no potential conflicts of interest with respect to the
research, authorship, and/or publication of this article. Additionally, the author(s) received no financial support for the research, authorship,
and/or publication of this article.

\subsection*{Data availability}

The conflict data are from the UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset v25.1 and the
UCDP Actor Dataset v25.1, both publicly available from the Uppsala Conflict Data
Program. Mineral exploration and discovery records are from S\&P Global Market
Intelligence, which is available under commercial licence and cannot be
redistributed by the authors. Replication code and all derived data that may be
shared will be deposited in a public repository on publication.
